The problem
Renting in Nigeria
runs on guesswork.

There is no credit bureau for tenants. No verified rental history. No way to prove you're a good tenant — or identify a bad one before it's too late.

For landlords & agents
Flying blind every time.
  • No way to verify a tenant's rental history before handing over keys
  • Rent defaults cost Nigerian landlords billions every year
  • The only defence is demanding 1–2 years' rent upfront
  • Word-of-mouth and gut instinct are the only tools available
For tenants & renters
Good tenants can't prove it.
  • Years of on-time payments vanish when you move — no record exists
  • Young professionals denied properties despite being perfectly reliable
  • Forced to pay enormous upfront deposits because trust has to be bought
  • No portable identity to carry from landlord to landlord
$2T
Nigeria's real estate market — the second largest in Africa — operates entirely on trust built by phone call. Voch is building the infrastructure that should have existed twenty years ago.
How it works
A trust layer for
Nigeria's rental market.

Voch is a two-sided mobile platform. Landlords screen. Tenants build their record. Everyone benefits from a shared, verified database.

01
Tenant creates their profile

Renters sign up, verify their NIN or BVN, confirm employment, and log their current tenancy. Every step adds points to their Voch Score.

Free for tenants
02
Landlord requests a check

The agent or landlord enters the tenant's name and phone number. The tenant gets a push notification and taps to consent. The check begins instantly.

Under 2 minutes
03
Score delivered, decision made

A Tenant Trust Score (0–100) is delivered with a full breakdown — identity, income, rental history, and any flags. Landlords approve or decline with confidence.

Approve or decline
